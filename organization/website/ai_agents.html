Yes. The course starts with AI and agent fundamentals before moving into advanced automation concepts.

Which automation tools are covered? ↓

The curriculum covers AI tools, Make, Zapier, APIs, Google Workspace automation and practical AI workflow systems.

Will I get a certificate? ↓

Yes. Students who successfully complete the course and required practical work can receive a certificate of completion.

Are practical projects included? ↓

Yes. The program is designed around practical AI agent and automation projects that can be added to your portfolio.
