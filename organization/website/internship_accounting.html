About The Training

Practical Accounting Internship

This Accounting Expert Internship Training Program is designed for students, freshers and beginners who want to develop practical accounting and office management skills.

During the training, candidates will practice accounting, Tally Prime, GST basics, MS Excel, Busy Accounting, billing, ledger management, voucher entry, documentation and financial reports.

Skills You Will Learn

Job-oriented practical skills

Basic Accounting

Debit, credit, journal, ledger and basic accounting concepts.

Tally Prime

Company creation, ledger, vouchers, inventory and accounting reports.

Busy Accounting

Business accounting, billing and inventory operations.

MS Excel

Formulas, tables, reports and accounting spreadsheets.

GST Basics

GST concepts, invoices, tax ledgers and transactions.

Financial Reports

Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, stock summary and reports.

Practical Training Activities

Create companies and maintain accounting masters.

Create and manage ledgers, groups and accounting records.

Practice sales, purchase, receipt and payment vouchers.

Prepare GST invoices and accounting transactions.

Prepare Excel-based accounting reports and statements.

Generate Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet and other financial reports.

Job Responsibilities

Your day-to-day responsibilities

Maintain daily accounting records and financial transactions.

Create and maintain ledgers, vouchers and journal entries.

Handle sales, purchase, receipt and payment entries.

Prepare invoices and maintain supporting documents.

Perform bank reconciliation and ledger reconciliation.

Work with GST-related accounting records and reports.

Prepare Excel reports, summaries and financial statements.

Verify accounting data and identify discrepancies.

Maintain confidentiality of financial information.

Coordinate with management and other departments.
