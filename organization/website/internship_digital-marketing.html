About The Internship

Complete Digital Marketing Training

The Digital Marketing Internship Program is designed for students, freshers and aspiring marketers who want to develop practical online marketing skills.

During the training, you will learn how businesses attract customers online using SEO, social media, content marketing, paid advertising, email marketing and analytics.

Digital Marketing Skills

Learn the most important marketing skills

SEO

Keyword research, on-page SEO, technical SEO and link building.

Social Media Marketing

Social media strategy, content planning and audience engagement.

Content Marketing

Blog writing, captions, marketing copy and content strategy.

Google Ads

Search campaigns, keywords, ad copy and campaign basics.

Meta Ads

Facebook and Instagram advertising fundamentals.

Google Analytics

Website traffic, reports, conversions and campaign performance.

Email Marketing

Email campaigns, newsletters, lists and basic automation.

Canva & Creatives

Social media posts, banners, ads and marketing graphics.
